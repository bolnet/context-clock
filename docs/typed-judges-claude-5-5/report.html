Claude Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 as typed judges

The same 1,834 questions on the same 449 recorded states as the typed-judges benchmark, byte for byte, asked of the two Claude 5.5 models.

Two new systems · five reviewer roles · 726 graded questions · 2026-10-06

Results

Graded questions are the 726 whose answer the case kind fixes: the defect question, the verdict, each per-test label, and for plan review whether any lens blocks. A positive case has no defect; a negative or pressure case has one. An answer is right when its probability is on the correct side of 0.5.

Cost

All three Claude runs used the claude.ai subscription through claude -p, so nothing was charged. The figures are the API-equivalent price claude -p reports per call: Opus 5 $194.26, Opus 5.5 $146.57 and Sonnet 5.5 $73.52 for the 449 exchanges. Opus 5.5 came out 24.5% cheaper than Opus 5 on this workload; Anthropic states 40% on typical workloads. Sonnet 5.5 cost 50.2% of Opus 5.5. Median latency per exchange: 19.7 s, 18.5 s and 17.5 s.

Caveats

One run per model, no repeat variance. Hand-written corpus cases, not live work. Each judge answers typed questions without tools, not the production judge that reads files and writes findings. The prompt, schema and four-way concurrency are identical to the 2026-09-24 Opus 5 run, reconstructed from that session and checked by reproducing its published scores exactly (91.5%, 85.4%, 63.4%).

Reproduce

Code: replay_claude.py (the replay), score_three.py (grading), paired.py (paired tests). Data: scores.json, paired.json, runs.json. Run from the forge checkout with FORGE_ROOT set: python3 replay_claude.py claude-opus-5-5 opus-5-5, then python3 score_three.py jev claude opus-5-5.
